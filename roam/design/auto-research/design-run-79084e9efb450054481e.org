* Objective

Design Note for Project auto-research, Run run-79084e9efb450054481e

Invariants:
- The system must autonomously conduct research tasks with minimal human intervention.
- Research outputs must be verifiable and reproducible.
- Data privacy and ethical guidelines must be strictly adhered to.

Actors:
- Autonomous Research Agent: Executes research tasks, gathers data, and synthesizes findings.
- User/Researcher: Defines research goals, reviews outputs, and provides feedback.
- Data Sources: External databases, publications, and repositories accessed by the agent.
- System Administrator: Maintains system integrity and updates.

Rejected Alternatives:
- Fully manual research process due to inefficiency and scalability issues.
- Purely heuristic-based research without data-driven validation due to lack of rigor.
- Open-ended research without defined invariants leading to inconsistent results.

This design ensures a balance between autonomy, reliability, and ethical compliance in the auto-research system.
