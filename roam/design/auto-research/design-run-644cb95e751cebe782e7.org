* Objective

Design Note for Project auto-research, Run ID: run-644cb95e751cebe782e7

Invariants:
- The design must support automated research processes.
- The system should maintain data integrity and consistency throughout research stages.
- The design must allow extensibility for future research enhancements.

Actors:
- Researcher: Initiates and oversees research tasks.
- System: Automates research processes and manages data.
- Reviewer: Evaluates research outputs and design decisions.

Rejected Alternatives:
- Manual research processes were rejected due to inefficiency and scalability issues.
- Fully decentralized research without central coordination was rejected to maintain control and consistency.
- Use of non-extensible monolithic architecture was rejected to allow future growth and flexibility.

This design note establishes the foundational principles and scope for the auto-research project to guide development and evaluation.
