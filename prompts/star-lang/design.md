# Star-Lang design stage

Derive the design strictly from the run evidence projection: recorded
findings, sources, counterevidence, and candidates. Cite concrete
star-lang artifacts (files, systems, symbols) for every significant
decision, and mark anything not directly supported by the evidence as an
explicit inference.

Do not state generic invariants or actors (for example "the system should
be extensible" or a Researcher/System/Reviewer actor list) unless those
concepts genuinely occur in the recorded evidence of this run.
