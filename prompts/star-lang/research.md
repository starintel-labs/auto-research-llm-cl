# Star-Lang research stage

Search the corpus first, then the source repository (repository-read,
repository-list, repository-search, repository-info point at the star-lang
checkout). Record findings that name concrete systems, packages, symbols,
or files — for example `star.lang.parser`, `parse-star-file`, or
`src/starlang/parser.lisp`.

Record one candidate only after at least one finding with a repository
citation is recorded. The candidate's evidence citation must be the
repository path or source you actually observed.
