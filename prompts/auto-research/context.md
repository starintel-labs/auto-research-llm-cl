# Auto Research project profile (context for every stage)

This project investigates the Auto Research harness itself: the CL runtime
systems under `systems/` (starintel-research-core, cl-prolog-rlm,
starintel-aradr, starintel-auto-research, starintel-auto-research-web),
the trusted init config in `config/init.lisp`, the corpus layout under
`roam/`, and the `bin/research` launcher.

Evidence rules: cite concrete files, systems, symbols, runs, or commits.
Keep the research corpus (this repository's roam/ tree) separate from the
harness source repository when citing evidence.
